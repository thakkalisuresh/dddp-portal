UPI handoff test

Which one opens your UPI app?

Tap each in order. After each tap, come back here and note what happened: the app opened, the page reloaded, or nothing at all. The amount is ₹1 — small enough to actually complete if one works.

Before you start: on the phone, open Settings → Apps → Google Pay → Open by default and make sure “Open supported links” is allowed. If that is switched off, Android refuses every link into the app and all eight of these will fail regardless of how they are built.
